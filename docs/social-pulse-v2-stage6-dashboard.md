# Social Pulse v2 — stage 6 dashboard note

Jacob, October 8 2026. This is an addition to the authoritative spec. Stage 6 only. Do not build it in earlier stages.

The dashboard headline is a sentiment-over-time graph from the 2023 announcement through now:

- Net sentiment, plus separate positive and negative lines.
- Trend direction on the graph.
- A narrative of how commenters' issues changed: what is emerging now versus about two years ago.
- Every narrative item is clickable through to the real comments it came from.

Do not put top-creator rankings on the dashboard. Do not put view, like, or follower metrics on the dashboard. Creator transcript quotes are allowed when they link to the post. Engagement is still collected internally for monitoring.
